A few details

Questions couples ask

Can you provide ceremony and reception audio?

Yes. Ceremony microphones, music, announcements, and reception sound can be planned as one seamless experience. Professional audio and lighting are available for events of up to 400 guests, depending on the venue and event requirements. I prepare with generally triple redundancy for critical equipment, so the celebration can keep moving if something unexpected happens.

Can we choose our own music?

Absolutely. Your favorites, must-plays, do-not-play list, and guest requests all help shape the evening.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular wedding dates fill early, so reach out as soon as you have your date and venue. I will confirm availability and next steps.

Do you travel outside Southern California?

Southern California is the primary service area. Travel beyond it may be available by arrangement.
