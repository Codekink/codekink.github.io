The process.
Clear and simple.

You've sent in your inquiry. Great. Now here's what to expect from me every step of the way.

01

Initial response

I'll reply to your inquiry within 24 hours with availability, next steps, and questions about your event. You can reach me via email, text, or phone.

02

Planning call

We'll schedule a conversation to dig into your vision, timeline, must-play songs, do-not-play list, and the small details that make your event feel like yours.

03

Ongoing communication

As your event approaches, we stay in touch. Updates, confirmations, vendor coordination, and any questions that come up—I'm available at any hour and respond within 24 hours.

04

Setup and coordination

I arrive early to coordinate with your venue and vendors, run sound checks, and confirm timing for special moments. Everything is locked in and ready.

05

Event day

I'm present, prepared, and reading the room so you can stay fully in your event. The day flows exactly as we planned.

06

Follow-up

After your event, I'll check in to make sure everything went the way you wanted and to answer any final questions.
